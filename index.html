Is the two-room cleaning really $99?

The offer applies to qualifying standard rooms. Additional services such as stairs, upholstery, specialty stain treatment or severe pet contamination may cost extra. Any additional work should be explained before it is performed.

Do you remove every stain?

No professional cleaner can responsibly guarantee that every permanent stain can be removed. We inspect problem areas and explain what can reasonably be expected.

Do I need to move my furniture?
